Strategic Thesis

Bigger than a print farm

Allternit Manufacturing is the physical production arm of the company. It produces our own hardware, monetizes excess capacity, and becomes a rapid prototyping lab — all while building software that can eventually be licensed to other factories.

High-Margin Services

Printing is the fulfillment layer. CAD, design, and the software that orchestrates production are the high-margin layers.

Decision

Risks & next steps

Key risks

  • Printer downtime → maintain spare parts and redundant capacity
  • Failed prints → camera monitoring + AI failure detection
  • Material moisture → dry cabinets + tracking
  • Tolerance issues → standardize profiles and qualify each printer
  • Regulatory (medical) → stay in industrial/consumer markets first

Immediate next steps

  • Approve master plan and Phase 1 budget
  • Validate CAD-as-a-Service pricing with 2–3 real estate or product clients
  • Select first printers and reserve physical space
  • Build internal part backlog for Allternit products
  • Identify 5 pilot B2B customers for printing or CAD
  • Choose CAD/CAM toolchain (FreeCAD + CadQuery + text-to-cad)
  • Prototype text-to-cad pipeline for one Allternit part
