What a real OS
must contain.

Linux, macOS, Windows, and Android—not agent-OS marketing—set the benchmark. The canonical inventory contains 48 responsibilities across the complete lifecycle. Allternit translates each one into an agentic kernel obligation.

01 · TRUST & BOOT

Start from known-good state

Root keys, signed and measured boot, hardware/runtime inventory, early authority, safe mode, and external recovery.

02 · KERNEL OBJECTS

Name and govern everything

Principals, workloads, steps, agents, models, artifacts, packages, leases, approvals, receipts, and explicit lifecycles.

03 · SCHEDULING & MEMORY

Allocate scarce intelligence

Structured concurrency, deadlines, budgets, model and executor placement, context paging, KV/VRAM pressure, cancellation.

04 · SYSCALLS & IPC

Mediate every transition

Typed capability calls, revocable leases, events, streams, locks, backpressure, deadlines, and attenuation across handoffs.

05 · DRIVERS & I/O

Treat the world as devices

Apps, APIs, browsers, models, robots, SaaS, and hardware use stable adapters with discovery, health, receipts, and hot removal.

06 · DATA & NETWORK

Durable truth, governed movement

Artifact namespaces, connectors, transactions, provenance, indexes, encrypted stores, egress rules, offline queues, backup.

07 · IDENTITY & SECURITY

Authority outside the model

Human, org, agent, package, device and runtime principals; purpose-bound leases; secrets; tenancy; isolation; deletion.

08 · SERVICES & PACKAGES

Operate software transactionally

Supervision, health, configuration, dependencies, signing, staged install/update/remove, rollback, revocation, compatibility.

09 · EXPERIENCE

Many projections, one truth

Desktop, Web, iOS, TUI, Office, API and voice share workload state, attention, approvals, interruption, accessibility.

10 · EVIDENCE & FAILURE

Know what actually happened

Journals, traces, receipts, costs, outcomes, watchdogs, checkpoints, replay, compensation, crash bundles, quarantine.

11 · VIRTUALIZATION & FLEET

Place work by risk

WASM, containers, microVMs, GUI VMs, remote runtimes, enrollment, attestation, sync, snapshots, placement, remote stop.

12 · ADMIN & CONFORMANCE

Make the constitution testable

Shell/API, debugger, policy simulation, package inspection, metrics, updates, migration, chaos tests, certification.

Weights evolve
by evidence.

The system optimizes reversibly before touching weights, then moves through adapters and distillation. Online agents never rewrite their active base model in place.

01 · RuntimePrompt/context packing, retrieval, caches, constrained decoding, routing, quantization and speculative execution.
02 · AdaptersTrain scoped LoRA/PEFT packages from consented, verified episodes; switch and remove independently.
03 · DistillationTrain a smaller specialist from filtered teacher traces; prove held-out quality, safety, cost and latency.
04 · Base releaseFull training occurs offline and becomes a new immutable model package with signature, evaluation and rollback.

Evolution has
a system call.

Every generated optimization crosses the same qualification pipeline. The candidate cannot alter its evaluator or erase the evidence used to judge it.

Observe

Collect verified outcomes, traces, cost, latency, energy, failures and environment facts.

Propose

Generate a kernel, harness, adapter, scheduler, driver or environment candidate.

Build

Compile reproducibly with declared dependencies, capabilities and rollback target.

Attack

Static checks, replay, fuzzing, adversarial fixtures, fault injection and escape tests.

Compare

Measure quality, safety, recovery, speed, spend, memory, energy and regression.

Shadow

Run without authority beside production; compare decisions and outcomes.

Canary

Limited signed activation with watchdog, health gates and automatic rollback.

Promote

Human or policy approval, immutable receipt, continuous monitoring and retained fallback.

Core rule: agents can search the space of better systems; they cannot redefine trust, grant themselves authority, lower their own acceptance threshold, replace recovery, or destroy the evidence against them.
